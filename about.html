About Me

About the author and this blog

Hi — I'm Mahesh Khanwalkar, a backend systems engineer. This blog is where I publish technical articles about large language models, machine learning, and open source technologies. Each article represents a project or concept I've explored in depth.

📝 My Approach This blog is a collection of my technical experiments and learnings. I aim to document the journey of learning AI and machine learning, sharing interesting learnings along the way.

1 Current Focus

I'm currently exploring efficient methods for fine-tuning large language models, particularly using QLoRA and Unsloth for memory-efficient training. My goal is to understand the practical aspects of making LLMs accessible and usable for various applications.

2 Blog Philosophy

This blog is intentionally simple and focused. I believe in documenting real experiments and practical learnings rather than theoretical discussions. Each article is a snapshot of my journey through the rapidly evolving field of AI and machine learning.
